Smart Rundown Prioritization (optional)

Off by default

The Daily Rundown can optionally use JEV, a judgment model from TypeSafe, to rate each unread email for category, whether it likely needs a reply, time sensitivity, and priority. Those ratings are passed to your AI provider as extra hints, so the rundown groups and orders mail more accurately.

This is off by default. When enabled, the date, sender, subject and a short body excerpt of each unread email are sent to api.typesafe.ai, which is a different company from the AI provider you configured above. Leave it off to keep the Daily Rundown working exactly as before.

Privacy & Data

Note: You are responsible for the selection and configuration of the third-party AI provider. Enabling Smart Rundown Prioritization additionally sends unread-email excerpts to TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai).
